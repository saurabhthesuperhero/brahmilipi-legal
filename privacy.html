Privacy Policy: Brahmi Lipi

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Brahmi Lipi: Learn Dhammalipi ("the app", package com.saikaizen.brahmilipi) teaches the Brahmi script. It is published by Saikaizen Innovations ("we", "us"). The app is free: it has no ads, no in-app purchases or payments, no account and no sign-in, and no server of ours. This page says exactly what the app does with data.

What stays on your phone

  • Your progress (lessons done, stars, review schedule, streak, points), your settings (language, writing input, sound, reminder time) and the name you may type to make a Brahmi name card are stored only on your phone.
  • What you draw while tracing letters is scored on the phone and is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Name cards and certificates are images made on your phone. They leave the phone only if you choose to share them.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes all of it.

What is sent over the internet: anonymous usage statistics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to learn which lessons and games are used and where learners get stuck, so we can improve the lessons. It sends usage events such as: a lesson or unit was started or finished, a game was played and its score, the score of a writing exercise, the chosen app language and writing input, a reminder was set, or a share/suggestion button was tapped. These come with device information (phone model, Android version, language, country), an app instance identifier created by Firebase, and approximate location derived from your IP address. They never contain your name, e-mail, the name you type in the app or anything you draw. The app sends no user id, and ad personalisation and advertising-ID collection are switched off. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and processed by Google under the Firebase privacy terms.

The app also uses Google Play's own In-App Review and In-App Updates windows. Updates are never forced: you can always close the update window.

We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Children

The app is suitable for learners of every age, but it is not designed for or directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone: no names, e-mail addresses or accounts.

Your choices

  • Reminder: switch it off in Settings at any time, or turn off the app's notifications in Android settings.
  • Delete everything the app stored: uninstall the app or clear its data. We hold no data that identifies you, so there is nothing for us to delete on request; Google keeps the anonymous statistics for its standard retention period.
