Brahmi Lipi: Learn Dhammalipi

Learn to read and write Brahmi (धम्मलिपी) in Marathi, Hindi or English.

Free forever: no ads, no in-app purchases, no account.

Short lessons teach every letter of the Brahmi script with stroke-order animations and tracing by finger or stylus, then vowel signs, conjuncts, words and real Ashokan inscriptions. Spaced review, games, a Devanagari to Brahmi converter and stories from history. Made for families, students and anyone who wants to read Dhammalipi.

Support and suggestions

Found a wrong letter shape, a mistake in a lesson, or have an idea? Email superprogrammersaurabh@gmail.com (Marathi, Hindi or English is fine). In the app you can also use Settings > Send a suggestion, which adds the app version for us. Please include your phone model if something does not work. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

  • Daily reminder: Settings > Daily reminder. It is off until you switch it on and choose the time.
  • Start again: Settings > Reset progress. Your progress is stored only on your phone.
  • Delete your data: uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.
